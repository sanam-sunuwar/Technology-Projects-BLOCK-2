## 🧠 5. Reflection Questions

### 5.1 What would happen if two PCs had the same IP address?
Two Pcs with the same IP address cause an IP conflict, which can disrupt network communication.

### 5.2 Why do we use a switch instead of directly connecting PCs together?
we use a switch to connect multiple PCs easily easily and  allow them to communicate with each other.

### 5.3 What does the subnet mask 255.255.255.0 indicate?
The subnet mask 255.255.255.0 indicates that the first three parts identify the network, while the last part identifies the host.
